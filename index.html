Next-Gen Learning.

An AI-driven educational platform featuring a peer-like study agent, adaptive reinforcement engine, and interactive avatar tutor.

Project Domain

Literature Survey & Research Gap

While current e-learning platforms provide static content, they lack personalized, adaptive interactions. EduMentor fills this gap by deploying an AI avatar tutor that adjusts to the user's learning pace.

Methodology & Technologies Used

Developed using a robust MERN stack architecture (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js), powered by localized Large Language Models (LLMs) and advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for highly accurate, contextual tutoring.
