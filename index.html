Our Research

Literature Survey

Current e-learning systems provide static, one-size-fits-all content. While platforms exist to host educational materials, they severely lack the ability to adapt to an individual student's cognitive pace. Recent studies show that students retain significantly more information when interacting with a responsive, peer-like entity rather than passively consuming text or video.

Research Gap

There is a distinct lack of personalized, real-time feedback mechanisms in traditional Learning Management Systems (LMS). Furthermore, existing tools rarely integrate advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to allow students to query their specific course materials interactively. The gap lies in the absence of a localized, context-aware AI tutor.

Research Problem

"How can we effectively increase student engagement, knowledge retention, and academic confidence through the use of a localized, context-aware AI educational platform?"

Project Objectives

Main Objective

To develop and deploy "EduMentor," an intelligent web-based educational platform that leverages locally hosted Large Language Models and MERN stack architecture to provide an adaptive, interactive, and highly personalized learning environment.

Studybuddy Agent

Develop a peer-like conversational agent to simulate collaborative study sessions.

Adaptive Reinforcement Engine

Implement an engine that dynamically adjusts quiz difficulty and learning paths based on performance.

Multiview Explanation Generator

Create a system that can explain complex concepts from multiple different angles and analogies.

AI Avatar Tutor

Integrate a visual, interactive AI avatar to provide a human-like tutoring presence.

Our Solution

EduMentor is a comprehensive cloud-native web application designed to bridge the gap between static learning and personalized tutoring. By utilizing PDF parsing and vector embeddings, the system ingests academic materials and uses cosine similarity searches to provide highly accurate, contextual answers. This ensures the AI does not hallucinate, but rather teaches directly from the syllabus.
